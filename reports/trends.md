## Reading the trend

- Missed calls per game went from 11.9 in 2021 to 9.9 in 2026.
- Before ABS, Statcast's average zone top moved by up to 0.93 inches between seasons. Part of any year-to-year change can come from the measurement, not the umpires, so treat small changes with care.
- 2026 uses a height-based ABS zone and the umpires' original calls (5,686 overturned calls restored), so compare it with earlier seasons cautiously.
